UFO/UAP Disclosure Archive — Interactive Map

uap-map.com is an independent, free, ad-free archive of 4,100+ UFO/UAP sighting and encounter events extracted from declassified records officially released by national governments. Every event on the map, timeline and report index links back to the original government document — the actual declassified PDF page, video or image — so any claim can be checked against its primary source.

The US Department of War collection includes records originating from the FBI, CIA, NASA, the Department of Energy, ODNI and the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO), published under the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE). New releases are indexed here shortly after they are published.

What you can do here

  • Interactive map — 4,100+ events on a world map; every marker opens a detail card with the event summary (English and Chinese) and a link to the source document, anchored to the exact page.
  • Timeline — the same events in chronological order, 1865–2026, filterable by date range, agency and country.
  • Report index — browse file by file: FBI case files, Project Sign / Blue Book era documents, astronaut debriefings, sensor videos and more.
  • Recent updates — what was newly added or corrected, release by release.

Method

Source PDFs are OCR-processed page by page; individual sighting events are then extracted, deduplicated and summarized with AI assistance (each summary is labeled with how it was produced), translated into Chinese, and geocoded. Only events with a city/town-level location are placed on the map — records that name only a country, region, ocean or orbit are kept searchable but not pinned to coordinates. Extraction mistakes are possible; every event links to its original document precisely so readers can verify.

Independence: this site is a personal, non-commercial project. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the U.S. Department of War or any other government agency. All indexed records are public-domain government releases; they are presented for research and education without any claim about the nature of the reported phenomena.

关于本站（中文简介）

uap-map.com 是一个独立、免费、无广告的解密档案索引项目，收录了 4,100 多起 UFO/UAP（不明异常现象）目击与接触事件，全部来自各国政府 正式解密公开的档案。地图、时间轴和报告索引中的每一起事件都直接链接到 原始政府文件——真实的解密 PDF 页面、视频或图像，方便逐条核对。

数据来源

  • 加拿大 — 解密 UFO 档案（FOIA 与 CIRVIS，约 8,700 页）：3,044 起
  • 美国战争部（Department of War） — UFO/UAP 解密发布 01–04 批（2026 年，含 FBI、CIA、NASA、能源部、AARO 等机构档案）：857 起
  • 新西兰国防军（1952–2009 年档案）：243 起
  • 澳大利亚国家档案馆：8 起
  • 巴西国家档案馆（1958 年特林达迪岛事件）：2 起

使用方式

  • 交互地图 — 世界地图上浏览全部事件，点击标记查看中英文摘要与原始文件链接（精确到页）。
  • 时间轴 — 按时间顺序浏览 1865–2026 年的事件，可按日期、机构、国家筛选。
  • 报告索引 — 按档案文件逐份浏览：FBI 案卷、蓝皮书时代文件、宇航员任务简报、军方传感器视频等。
  • 最近更新 — 查看每一批新增与修订的记录。

方法与声明

原始 PDF 逐页 OCR 后，由 AI 辅助提取、去重并生成中英文事件摘要（每条摘要都标注了生成方式）， 再进行地理编码。只有具体到城市/城镇级别的地点才会在地图上打点；仅提到国家、地区、海域或轨道的 记录保留可检索但不标坐标。提取过程可能存在错误，因此每条事件都精确链接到原始文件供核对。

本站为个人非商业项目，与美国战争部或任何政府机构没有任何隶属或背书关系。 所有收录档案均为公有领域的政府公开文件，仅供研究与学习使用，本站不对所报告现象的性质作任何判断。
